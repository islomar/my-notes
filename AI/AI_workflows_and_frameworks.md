## Tools

- [Elephant-Goldfish Model (Google)](https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model)
  - Open-source plugin for designing and reviewing software with agents
  - Develop ideas with context and review documents with fresh eyes. EGM is an open-source plugin for AI assistants.
  - Elephant holds your project context and drafts the specification.
    Goldfish reviewers examine the draft and referenced source material without the earlier conversation, helping expose unstated assumptions and blind spots.
    You make the architectural trade-offs and approve the specification before implementing.
- [C.R.I.I.S.P.](https://github.com/richardedens/crissp)
  - The agentic SDLC framework for Claude Code: AI colleagues build, guards prove, a person decides.
- [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/openspec)  
  - Spec-driven development (SDD) for AI coding assistants.
- [GitHub Spec Kit](https://github.github.com/spec-kit/)
  - Spec Kit gives your coding agent structured processes that keep intent and evidence ahead of implementation.
  - Start with Spec-Driven Development (SDD), bug fixing, or idea assessment.
  - Run a process step by step, automate it, or shape one of your own — including processes beyond software development.
